01 / ABOUT

Curiosity, built into hardware.

I enjoy the whole path from understanding how something moves to making a physical system that can do it. My work sits at the intersection of robot mechanisms, actuation, biomechanics, and space robotics.

At Carnegie Mellon, I study how mechanical advantage and morphology influence performance. I’m especially interested in designing mobility and manipulation systems for places where conventional robots struggle.
