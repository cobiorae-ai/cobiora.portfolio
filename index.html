01 / ABOUT

Curiosity, built into hardware.

Originally from California, I moved to Pennsylvania to study engineering at Swarthmore College, where I earned a B.S. in Engineering with minors in Computer Science and Astronomy. I’m now pursuing an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, which I expect to complete in spring 2027. I plan to pursue a Ph.D. in mechanical or aerospace engineering beginning in fall 2027.

I enjoy understanding how things move and turning those insights into physical systems. At CMU’s RoboMechanics Lab, my research explores mechanical advantage, actuation, and biomechanics-inspired design. I’m especially interested in mobility and manipulation systems that help robots adapt to unfamiliar environments, including planetary surfaces.

I’ve also spent my summers at The Aerospace Corporation, working first on engineering software and later on electromechanical systems. Outside of engineering, the arts have always been important to me. I played saxophone in a jazz band for eight years, performed in school and public settings, and still love going to see musicals and local theater productions.
