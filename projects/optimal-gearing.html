PROJECT OVERVIEW / 01

Engineering question

How do organisms use limb geometry and leverage to match available force and motion to a task—and what can engineered actuators learn from this?

My contribution

I analyze musculoskeletal models to study joint moment arms, lever geometry, and mechanical advantage. Related actuation work investigates how gearing changes the performance of different soft actuator concepts under a common motion task.

Methods & tools

  • Extract kinematics and moment-arm information from OpenSim models.
  • Compare geometric leverage and force-/displacement-based gearing measures.
  • Build Python analysis and visualization workflows to examine design trends.
