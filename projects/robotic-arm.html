Engineering question

How can a multi-degree-of-freedom arm be designed to balance reach, weight, actuator torque, fabrication constraints, and controllability?

My contribution

For my senior engineering capstone, I worked on joint design, motor selection, and the tradeoffs between arm geometry and payload demands. The process involved iterating from early sketches to CAD and fabrication.

Methods & tools

  • Estimate joint torque requirements from link weight and geometry.
  • Compare actuator choices and mechanical packaging constraints.
  • Iterate components through CAD, prototyping, and fabrication.
