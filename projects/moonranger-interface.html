Lunar Rover–Lander Interface

Mechanical concepts for integrating, protecting, and deploying a compact rover from a lunar lander.

Space Robotics Mechanical Integration Fusion 360 CAD
A reliable start for lunar mobility.
PROJECT OVERVIEW / 01

Engineering question

How can a lander interface protect a rover during transit and then enable a practical deployment sequence on the lunar surface?

My contribution

In a space robotics course project, I served as a mechanical lead on a lunar rover–lander interface concept, including structural packaging and a garage-like enclosure with opening components.

Methods & tools

  • Develop CAD concepts for the enclosure and deployment interfaces.
  • Evaluate packaging, clearances, and hinge or door arrangements.
  • Communicate the design using assembly views and motion illustrations.
