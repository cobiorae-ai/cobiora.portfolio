Wind-Farm Wake Analysis

Studying how upstream turbine wakes affect wind conditions and downstream energy production using field measurements and Python.

Fluid MechanicsPythonLIDAR / SCADAData Analysis
How do turbine wakes change energy production?
PROJECT DETAILS / 01

Overview

Wind farms can produce renewable electricity at scale, but upstream turbines alter the flow reaching downstream machines. During a summer research fellowship in Stanford University’s Computational Fluid Physics Laboratory, I investigated how these wake interactions affect turbine performance. I worked with LIDAR and supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) measurements from a wind-farm site and used Python to examine power curves, compare upwind and downwind turbines, and investigate anomalous operating points. This data-driven analysis helped characterize possible wake-related performance differences and provided experience connecting fluid mechanics to operational measurements.
